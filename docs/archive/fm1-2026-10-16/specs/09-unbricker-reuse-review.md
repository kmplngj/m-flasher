## Hazards that prevent wholesale reuse

1. **Different bounds at different layers.** Native `_check_mutable` prohibits the head but permits mutations up to `0x100000`, including data outside the APP. Its ordinary restore calls currently request only `[0x4000,0x93000)`, so this is an unsafe reusable helper boundary, not a claim that nominal restore overwrites data. Transporter `write_sector` enforces exactly one aligned 4 KiB APP sector. Retain both our operation-specific host gate and the board gate for every operation/retry/rollback. [Native helpers](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_unbrick.py#L522-L573), [serial gate](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_transporter_recover.py#L251-L262).
2. **Layout/family checks are narrower than our contract.** Parser `ufw_flash` selects the last type-0 entry and checks header/table CRC, file bounds and minimum APP length; it does not perform our duplicate/overlap/chip/layout checks. `classify` treats numerical `FM-1_0xx` identities as official-style and most other numerical identities as Felucca-family. APP-hash equality alone selects verified V15 while whole-package differences are informational. These are not exact source→[local artifact location omitted] qualifications. Transporter planning warns on head differences and ignores package bytes after `0x93000`; our profiles must refuse unsupported layouts and head/data incompatibility. [Parser/classifier](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_unbrick.py#L237-L358), [plan](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_transporter_recover.py#L1002-L1043).
3. **Backup durability is useful but incomplete.** Both save paths use exclusive creation, file fsync and reread after two equal reads. They do not enforce two independently stored copies, directory fsync, strict source/session metadata, or backup revalidation before each destructive action. Transporter `recover` captures two reads, but `restore --ref` only loads a supplied full-size file; that command cannot establish its double-read origin or current durability. [Native backup](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_unbrick.py#L986-L1012), [serial persistence](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_transporter_recover.py#L942-L979), [restore/recover](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_transporter_recover.py#L1361-L1399).
4. **Verification differs by route.** Native restore erases/programs the whole APP, compares only the APP, and retries the entire APP once on mismatch. It does not prove untouched flash equality. Serial restore writes only changed APP sectors and compares the full expected image before return, which is the stronger reference. However its fresh-reference comparison requires only `[0,0x93000)` equality, reports changed tail data and uses that new tail in its expected image. Our immutable full-backup baseline must not silently accept those changes. [Native verifier](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_unbrick.py#L1037-L1056), [serial verifier](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_transporter_recover.py#L1046-L1116).
5. **Failure/session handling is not an agent recovery contract.** Both command wrappers close handles in `finally`, including failure. Native danger text recommends rerunning restore; the wizard also advises starting again after a failed write. Whether closing loses the physical held loader depends on bridge/loader behavior and is unproven here. Our qualification must specify keepalive, held-session cancellation and safe teardown; a generic rerun cannot become automatic resume or rollback. Upstream discovery also probes candidates and returns the first serial `PONG`; keep our explicit dual-CDC selection and session identity. Native `--i-know`, `--force-write` and confirmation-skipping options must not become qualification bypasses. [Handle closure](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_unbrick.py#L1092-L1098), [serial discovery](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_transporter_recover.py#L487-L532), [serial closure](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_transporter_recover.py#L1306-L1312), [wizard failure advice](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_transporter_recover.py#L1858-L1917).

A restore-to-V15 is not a general rollback/data migration. Neither script proves that the original snapshot is bootable, declares a qualified reverse transition, or establishes restored preset compatibility. They deliberately avoid the head, but preserving data before boot does not constrain writes made later by the running firmware. Retain FM1-015's explicit historical-known-good and fresh-current-backup requirements and FM1-023's distinction between recovery snapshots and preset exports.

## Parser correction and independent tests

The extraction fix is pinned to **`f6e1bc24a6527f6aa85d570d28322fa67a9dde26`**, **2026-10-07 22:51:30 UTC**. The earlier slice started at logical `0x4000`; with the example flash entry at logical `0x400`, physical APP `0x4000` actually starts at logical `0x4400`. The correct rule is **directory entry offset + physical APP offset**, never a universal hard-coded `0x400` adjustment. A following commit changed V15 recognition to the correct APP-region digest. Our parser and independent corpus already cover shifted and non-default offsets. [Correction commit](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/commit/f6e1bc24a6527f6aa85d570d28322fa67a9dde26), [recognition correction](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/commit/eca8f0b06a7302043288be99f9703603dfb6fe89).
