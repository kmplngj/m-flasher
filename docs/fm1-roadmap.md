# FM-1 roadmap and transferred research

Updated 2026-10-16. This fork reuses M-Flasher’s Rust CLI and GUI, package/layout analysis, full raw backup, basic restore, sector-differential writes, fake NOR tests and platform packaging. The issues track concrete gaps rather than recreating those capabilities. The machine-readable [migration map](fm1-migration-map.json) covers all 34 prior FM1 goals.

The minimal read-only RAM reader has a separate **experimental CLI integration**. Two independent offline builds reproduce the same 1,456-byte image; hosted fake-core/MMIO checks, 37 artifact mutation tests and independent static review pass. After the [small read/return observation](evidence/fm1-reader-check-2026-10-16.json), the owner's separately authorized V15/macOS task completed two fresh equal 1 MiB raw passes, two protected reopened copies and final held-session checks, followed by manual cold return and a fresh `FM-1_015` reply. See the [complete-backup CLI](fm1-raw-backup.md) and [sanitized full observation](evidence/fm1-complete-raw-backup-2026-10-16.json). Both chosen copies are on one filesystem, so physical-media independence remains unverified. Default builds remain inactive; other devices/platforms, writes and recovery remain unqualified. Ordinary development still permits local files and injected fakes only. Published issues do not grant hardware authority.

## Delivery sequence

First establish the exact reader artifact (#3), strict transport (#4) and host profile (#5), then running-stock identity/entry and a small read/return qualification (#6–#8). Only afterward activate two fresh full raw passes (#9) and protected independent copies (#10). Offline seams (#2) and saved-file work can progress independently of physical gates. The migration map separates offline prerequisites from integration inputs and physical activation: #9 requires physical #8 only for activation, and #11 consumes #10 receipts when available without requiring a prior hardware capture.

Saved-file diagnosis (#11) supplies the inputs for exact data/transition policy (#13), which in turn qualifies selective archives (#12). Application-only verification (#14) also depends on the accepted capture/storage and transition contracts. Retain MIDI OTA (#15) only if selected. Writer/recovery qualification (#16) and agent physical cycles (#17) require an external spare/bench, never the sole owner unit. A selectively enabled small CLI (#18) need not wait for optional writer/agent work. Web (#19) remains step 2.

## Research ledger retained by the fork

These are source-bound findings transferred from the reference research, not newly performed hardware observations. Primary links below identify the inspected source revisions; downloaded proprietary firmware and private captures are not published here.

**Identity is advisory.** A separately authorized historical identity-only CLI observation reported `FM-1_015`, with an independently checked canonical 41-byte wire/34-byte decoded reply. It established neither installed-image integrity nor ROM entry, flash/chip identity, raw capture or return after RAM execution. No personal endpoint/device identifier is needed to retain that finding. The reviewed request is `F0 00 32 45 00 00 00 40 7F F7`; it differs from the update trigger. The [protocol/parser](https://github.com/AL-255/FM-1-RE/blob/ec832f281ca165a7d5e0722985f3acb1465046d5/tools/fm1_ota.py) and [external V15 macOS bench](https://github.com/ip2k/lunar-modulator/blob/bfa042446f36c8647efddc6d76b06753d2a5b612/notes/2026-09-06-bench.md) corroborate the format. Firmware-controlled numbers are not stock/custom classifiers: an external `FM-1_092` return and shared custom 9xx strings prevent that inference. This historical observation does not qualify this fork’s future identity adapter.

**Exact V15 entry is a mode change.** The manufacturer-hosted [FM-1.fwsc](https://yms-file-store.oss-cn-hongkong.aliyuncs.com/software/firmware/FM-1.fwsc), linked by its [download page](https://www.m-vave.com/download), was bound at 699,956 bytes and SHA-256 `db1642b2b6fa5c2cccb11ffd13878068bb28601678d3644049f99dc40e7edb8a`. Static inspection resolves logical SysEx `F0 22 24 35 7D F7` and USB-MIDI bytes `04 F0 22 24 07 35 7D F7` through the V15 receiver to RAM `0x01C026A4`: interrupt/lock changes, a `usb_update_mode` mailbox at `0x01C7FD80` and a P33 reset request. An earlier V13 `0xFFC02532` annotation was file-relative, not an absolute mask-ROM call. The [Transporter entry source](https://github.com/kurogedelic/FM-1-transporter/blob/a632d923203170e3a08565f4056dbb3fd3d65ca5/src/pio_host.c#L99-L117) and [reset analysis](https://github.com/ip2k/lunar-modulator/blob/bfa042446f36c8647efddc6d76b06753d2a5b612/notes/2026-10-05-softkey-efuse.md#L42-L80) are primary corroboration. Static macOS packetizer/receiver compatibility does not establish the current loaded driver, quiet queue, selected delivery, matching reset consumer, autonomous effects or normal return. The adjacent `7F` OTA path is not a fallback; do not install V15 or edit identity merely to enter this route.

**Package, logical image and raw flash are different objects.** The exact V15 package contains a 602,112-byte type-0 `flash.bin`, not an entire independently identified 1 MiB device. Its raw encrypted APP `[0x4000,0x93000)` is 585,728 bytes, SHA-256 `6edf3c37fb5bbbc33607c89375ee024d5477c17914d72221c8c68e58a8255686`. Its decoded application is a different representation. Preserve an independent oracle for the historical `0x400` extraction shift rather than adding a package offset to a physical read. V15 also has a valid empty type-2, NUL-padded `info.log` at the next payload boundary: permit only the exact zero-CRC/reserved/ordinal/bounds case, not arbitrary empty raw regions or markers inside payloads. Inspect the existing [formats](https://github.com/kmplngj/m-flasher/blob/9d962747437d92cbab594c2320b586d162c6572d/src/formats.rs) and [layout analysis](https://github.com/kmplngj/m-flasher/blob/9d962747437d92cbab594c2320b586d162c6572d/src/boards.rs); transfer only missing regressions. No downloaded package, preset archive or parsed application can replace the connected unit’s pre-install raw snapshot.

**Reader routes have different requirements.** Original 24,064-byte vendor helpers can use retained A/B configuration, a ROM initializer at `0x01E00428`, timer/state and supplied callbacks; bare-ROM FD07 can transform source memory before sending/restoring it. The [FD07 protocol warning](https://github.com/kagaimiq/jl-uboot-tool/blob/adb3f18889e88ac512ce0a3c4d8cc3d3cb30696a/docs/usb-loader-v2.md#L176-L193) does not prove selected success/error restoration or active RAM ownership. A distinct official 30,720-byte [SDK helper](https://gitee.com/Jieli-Tech/fw-AC79_AIoT_SDK/blob/d179b4484759423312073f5fbb232501aa491047/cpu/wl82/tools/wl82loader.bin), also retained by [Melodee](https://github.com/keremimo/melodee/blob/0c21568a03626f1240229f7f368cfc2a7b09bafb/assets/recovery/wl82loader.bin), has different A/B/state and 88-byte metadata. Its local mode-2 read path does not require FC0A, but mode-4 startup and FC0A protection handling can reach status programming; optional FC0C cleanup also matters. Those addresses, metadata and claims cannot be transferred between helpers.

**The Lunar patch is positive evidence with bounded limits.** The pinned [configuration patch](https://github.com/ip2k/lunar-modulator/blob/53fa05b278d217476b7e92ba24d6d5dd1e1e1010/tools/jieli/loader_config_profile.py) changes 16 bytes of the 24,064-byte helper and removes optional A/B pre-read/config transforms; candidate encoded SHA-256 is `fe6d1bd36c8ffe4674e4a05ee748fe87480b5867fac58230b14ff089d411012c`. Local positive FD05/256 refreshes the idle counter without FC0A, and fixed loader FD07 copies 84 bytes of metadata. However, startup still calls `0x01E00428`; its effects and supplied stack/interface/hook/send ownership remain unqualified. This helper actually reads 32 interface bytes despite three functional words. The receive target is unused, and the zero terminator is needed only through the final lookup before that ROM call. Do not demand unused receiver bodies or preservation of dead padding. Send bytes must be consumed before scratch reuse; callback return alone does not prove that ordering.

The exact-helper [external physical report](https://github.com/ip2k/lunar-modulator/blob/53fa05b278d217476b7e92ba24d6d5dd1e1e1010/notes/2026-10-11-fixed-loader-physical-backup.md) reports **two equal complete 1 MiB reads**, with 8,192 FD05 requests of 256 bytes. This materially supports usability. Its sequence includes **515 FC0A**, one FC14 and **no fixed 84-byte loader FD07**; private captures/allocations were not independently inspected. It does not establish the exact no-FC0A/status-write subset or this fork’s M1 route. Later return evidence followed application rewrites on Linux and is not unchanged-stock backup-only return on V15/macOS. Neither prior backup nor a write/restore experiment is a prerequisite to a narrowly qualified first read.

**Foreign ROM artifacts do not close the selected contract.** Official paths named WL82 contained an empty [mask stub](https://github.com/Jieli-Tech/fw-Bootloader/blob/38e6ff29b990ea3fe3819fb621bb882812abd613/user_boot/cpu/wl82/output/maskrom_stubs.ld) and a 131,072-byte [rom.image](https://github.com/Jieli-Tech/fw-Bootloader/blob/38e6ff29b990ea3fe3819fb621bb882812abd613/user_boot/cpu/wl82/output/rom.image) identified as BR28-identical, not the matching selected WL82 ROM. An empty [SDK wrapper](https://github.com/Jieli-Tech/fw-Bootloader/blob/ff3b9299d1b94d834b995fd858b3f75e2faa9840/user_boot/include_lib/cpu/wl82/mask_api.h) is not proof that the binary call is a no-op. The SDK linker’s external-flash `rom` at `0x02000120` is not an immutable-ROM allocation map. Unchanged archive/search repeats cannot replace matching implementation or scoped external evidence; complete ROM reconstruction is not required.

**M-Flasher is a different source-defined route.** The pinned [loader source](https://github.com/kmplngj/m-flasher/blob/9d962747437d92cbab594c2320b586d162c6572d/loader/loader.c) initializes SPI locally with JEDEC9F and reads raw03 without direct `0x01E00428` or FC0A. Its exact A/B prebuilt artifacts were each 3,496 bytes. The full upstream image also has destructive commands, clock/peripheral extras, unbounded waits and unknown-command ROM fallback. The minimal M1 derivative removes those paths rather than merely hiding host buttons. Vendor A/B pre-read, 32-byte debug and 84/88-byte metadata/timer assumptions do not apply to M1. Actual ROM entry/hook/send, supplied stack and code/state/CBW/answer/buffer ownership, chip effects, timing and return still require selected evidence. Its host [constructor](https://github.com/kmplngj/m-flasher/blob/9d962747437d92cbab594c2320b586d162c6572d/src/jl.rs) currently probes FC0A, can fall back to SDK and retries; [BOT](https://github.com/kmplngj/m-flasher/blob/9d962747437d92cbab594c2320b586d162c6572d/src/usb.rs) accepts short IN/ignores residue and performs recovery traffic. Strict host work must address those concrete gaps without replacing the existing USB library by default.

**USB-C and electrical USB_KEY are separate routes.** A qualified direct-computer ROM read need not use RP2040; an installed app’s musical archive and MIDI installer are also distinct from physical raw export. When neither application nor ROM is reachable, an external electrical activator may be needed. [Dedicated USB_KEY notes](https://kagaimiq.github.io/jielie/isp/usb/usb-key.html) assign D+ to clock and D− to data; the older [entry guide](https://github.com/kagaimiq/jl-uboot-tool/blob/adb3f18889e88ac512ce0a3c4d8cc3d3cb30696a/docs/how-to-enter-uboot.md#usb_key) reverses the prose while its waveform toggles D+ as clock. Bind exact activator source/pin routing/waveform/ACK/handoff on an isolated external setup; never try both mappings on the sole owner unit. An emergency dongle is not a mandatory prerequisite for qualified software USB-C backup.

**Keep evidence and authority separate.** Source inspection, hosted fake tests, artifact build/digest, target startup, completed SPI DMA, actual USB data/CSW, fresh full acquisition, durable reopened independent files and original-stock return are separate facts. A successful send return, 100% progress, two copies of one cached buffer or a saved-file receipt cannot bridge them. Physical writes, interruption/recovery experiments and autonomous flash/boot loops belong on a separately enrolled external spare, never the sole owner unit. Preserve the head and unapproved data, refuse unknown edges, and stop after ambiguity without automatic reset/reflash/rollback. RAM execution changes mode and can hang; absence of flash writes provides no zero-risk or universal no-brick guarantee.

## Earlier recovery and source-reuse findings

These are retained source observations and attributed external reports, not newly reproduced hardware results or copied implementations.

**Extraction is directory-relative, not a universal shift.** The pinned [Unbricker parser](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_unbrick.py#L188-L365) and its [correction commit](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/commit/f6e1bc24a6527f6aa85d570d28322fa67a9dde26) establish `directory-entry offset + physical APP offset`. An example entry at logical `0x400` maps physical APP `0x4000` to logical `0x4400`; adding a universal `0x400` would be wrong for other directory offsets. Preserve independent non-default-offset fixtures. Its [mock read](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_transporter_recover.py#L404-L418) can return unstable bytes with matching transport CRC: two fresh reads must still disagree and permit no writes. [Saved-dump diagnosis tests](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/tests/test_transporter.py#L329-L424) accept a mixed old/new image with an intact loader/update record; that saved label is not proof of cold resume, bootability or known-entry recovery.

**Native and serial verification differ.** Unbricker’s [native restore](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_unbrick.py#L1037-L1056) erases/programs and verifies the APP only, then retries the whole APP on mismatch. The [serial route](https://github.com/Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker/blob/b8c8e8afbcba3e45b262b3b624ab9d0244b92628/fm1_transporter_recover.py#L1046-L1116) writes changed APP sectors and checks a full expected image, but its fresh-reference comparison requires only `[0,0x93000)` equality and accepts changed tail bytes into the new baseline. Retain immutable whole-flash backup binding, preservation of all untouched regions, full held-session verification and terminal failure rather than adopting stale-tail acceptance or full-job retry.

**Single-bank incidents limit recovery claims.** The pinned [INSTALL-COMPAT incident](https://github.com/Quixotic7/ChoralRootFM1/blob/e8efc993c986f8d7661c515ebfc8c46c6e77057c/docs/INSTALL-COMPAT.md) reports an interrupted mixed image with 33 new and 110 old sectors, intact head/staged loader, no USB after cold boot, and later Transporter recovery. An intact record was not a demonstrated cold-resume path. [BOOT-SAFETY](https://github.com/Quixotic7/ChoralRootFM1/blob/e8efc993c986f8d7661c515ebfc8c46c6e77057c/docs/BOOT-SAFETY.md) reports successful same-stub cable-pull rescue on one unit, while first installation or replacement of the stub retains 16 uncovered cut points in its model. These are scoped positive/negative reports, not a universal zero-brick guarantee or permission to repeat the experiment on the sole owner unit.
